01 Research Content

What does the paper study?

研究内容

This study mainly examines how the digital economy promotes agricultural new quality productive forces.

It not only investigates whether the digital economy has a positive effect, but also explores the mechanisms behind this effect, regional differences, and whether the impact changes under different levels of economic development and urbanization.

In short, the paper answers three key questions: Can the digital economy empower agriculture? How does this happen? Where is the effect more obvious?

02 Theoretical Framework

How does the digital economy empower agriculture?

理论基础

The paper explains the empowering role of the digital economy through four aspects.

These include digital infrastructure, digital industrialization, industrial digitalization, and digital innovation potential.

It also argues that digital literacy is an important transmission mechanism. In other words, the digital economy can improve agricultural productivity both directly and indirectly by strengthening workers’ ability to use digital tools and technologies.

Chinese Support

文章从数字基础设施、数字产业化、产业数字化和数字创新潜力四个方面解释数字经济的赋能作用。

例如，数字基础设施可以提升数据收集和智能决策能力，电商与数字平台可以拓宽农产品销售渠道， 数字技术还能提升生产、加工与销售各环节效率。

同时，作者特别强调人才数字素养这一传导机制， 也就是数字经济除了直接促进农业发展，还可以通过提升从业者数字技能产生间接影响。

03 Research Design

What data and methods are used?

研究设计

The study uses provincial panel data from 31 regions in China from 2012 to 2022.

It constructs two comprehensive indices: one for the digital economy and one for agricultural new quality productive forces.

The authors use the entropy weight method to calculate the indices, the two-way fixed effects model to test the overall impact, and the mediation model to examine the role of digital literacy.

Simple explanation:
Entropy weight method = assigns weights according to data variation.
Two-way fixed effects = controls for provincial differences and time effects.
Mediation model = tests whether digital literacy is an intermediate channel.
Chinese Support

研究样本为2012—2022年中国31个省级地区数据，构建数字经济和农业新质生产力两个综合指标体系。

农业新质生产力主要从农业劳动者、农业劳动对象和农业劳动生产资料三个维度进行衡量。

实证方法上，文章使用熵权法、双向固定效应模型和中介效应模型， 并进一步进行稳健性、异质性、分维度和门槛效应分析。

04 Empirical Analysis

What do the empirical results show?

实证分析

The empirical results show that the digital economy has a significant positive effect on agricultural new quality productive forces.

This result remains stable after different robustness tests. Meanwhile, digital literacy plays a partial mediating role.

Regional analysis shows that the effect is strongest in western China, followed by eastern China and then central China.

The threshold regression further shows that the positive effect becomes stronger when economic development and urbanization reach certain levels.

Mediating Effect 人才数字素养发挥部分中介作用，说明既有直接影响，也有间接影响。
Regional Pattern 区域效应表现为：西部 ＞ 东部 ＞ 中部。
Threshold Effect 经济发展水平和城镇化水平达到一定程度后，促进作用会更强。
06 / 06
05 Research Conclusions

What is the final conclusion?

研究结论

Overall, the paper concludes that the digital economy is an important driver of agricultural new quality productive forces.

However, its impact is not uniform. It varies according to digital literacy, regional conditions, economic development, and urbanization.

Therefore, the authors suggest improving rural digital infrastructure, strengthening digital skills, adopting differentiated regional policies, and promoting deeper integration of digital technologies into agricultural production and management.

Chinese Support

总体来看，文章认为数字经济是推动农业新质生产力发展的重要力量， 但这种作用会受到数字素养、地区差异、经济发展水平和城镇化水平的影响。

因此，作者建议继续完善农村数字基础设施，提高农业从业者数字素养， 根据东中西部不同发展条件制定差异化政策，并推动数字技术更深层次融入农业生产和管理全过程。
